LifeOps Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

LifeOps is a personal productivity application that helps users manage tasks, habits, goals, and profile information.

Information We Collect

When you use LifeOps, we may collect:

  • Email address used for account authentication.
  • Display name, pronouns, and timezone when provided.
  • Tasks, notes, due dates, and completion status.
  • Habits and habit completion information.
  • Goals, descriptions, progress, areas, and deadlines.

How We Use Information

We use this information to provide authentication, synchronize your productivity data, operate the LifeOps service, maintain security, and improve the application.

Data Storage

LifeOps uses Supabase for authentication and cloud data storage. Your authenticated workspace data is associated with your LifeOps account.

Local Storage

LifeOps may use local device or browser storage for application settings, onboarding state, and compatibility-related local data.

Data Security

LifeOps uses authenticated access controls to help restrict workspace information to the account that owns it. Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the service.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their LifeOps account and associated personal data. Requests can be made using the contact information provided with the service.

Children's Privacy

LifeOps is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
